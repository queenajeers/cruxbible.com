How it works

Put first things first.

Crux Bible turns the phone that pulls you away into a gentle nudge toward God’s Word.

  1. 1

    Lock what distracts you

    Choose the apps that steal your mornings: social, news, games. From the time you wake up, Crux Bible holds them with Apple’s Screen Time.

  2. 2

    Spend time with God

    Open today’s lesson: a guided start, the passage, the story in visual cards, a short quiz and a closing prayer. About fifteen minutes.

  3. 3

    Step into your day

    Finish the lesson and your apps unlock. Your streak grows, and tomorrow the journey carries on from where you left off.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Crux Bible?

Crux Bible is a visual Bible app for iPhone. It walks you through the whole Bible in 365 days, one short lesson each morning, explained with clear pictures, maps, timelines and narration. While you read, the apps that distract you stay locked.

What is a visual Bible?

A visual Bible explains Scripture with pictures as well as words. In Crux Bible every lesson walks through the passage in illustrated story cards: maps of where it happened, timelines of when, and diagrams of how it all fits together, so the story is easy to follow and hard to forget.

When can I get Crux Bible?

Crux Bible is coming soon to iPhone. We’re putting the finishing touches on the first lessons of the journey now. Leave your email and we’ll let you know the day it’s out.

How does the app lock work?

Crux Bible uses Apple’s Screen Time. You choose the apps to lock and the time you wake up. Each day at that time they lock, and they open again once you’ve finished the day’s lesson. Apple keeps your choices private on your iPhone.

How long does a lesson take?

Most mornings take about fifteen minutes: a guided start, the passage, the story in visual cards, a short quiz and a closing prayer.

What if I miss a day?

The journey waits for you. You pick up exactly where you left off, so no part of the story is ever skipped.

Is it only for people who already know the Bible?

Not at all. Crux Bible is made for anyone who wants to understand the Bible, whether you’re opening it for the very first time or you’ve read it for years.

Which Bible translations are included?

You can read the whole Bible in seven versions: the New International Version (NIV), New American Standard Bible (NASB), King James Version (KJV), Berean Standard Bible (BSB), World English Bible (WEB), American Standard Version (ASV) and World English Bible Catholic Edition.
